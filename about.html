Our Team

Julio

Artist, he is the blacksmith of our kingdom. With his strength and dedication, he forges the foundations of our office, ensuring that everything is in order and that the team has the necessary tools to shine. Each of his hammer blows is one more step towards the solidity of our dream.

Ian

Our CEO, he is the chef who had the original idea, the vision of transforming reading into something delicious. Ian is the engine that drives the project, always looking for ways to move everything forward, bringing the team together and making sure we have the tools... and the laughs! Because yes, he's a joker, but his energy and dedication are the fire that keeps our creative kitchen always burning.

Fran

Our producer, has always been a book devourer. Since he was a child, you would find him with a book in his hand, lost in a thousand adventures, dreaming of dragons, pirates, and distant worlds. He has been the captain on this journey, guiding us with his passion for reading.

Andrés

Our game designer. With his overflowing imagination, he creates fun mechanics. As a child, he used to build cardboard mazes for his friends, and now he designs experiences that invite young readers to immerse themselves in each game.

Margarita

Our illustrator, always had colored pencils in one hand and a book in the other. Her favorite was Kika Superbruja and her drawings are full of the colors and magic she saw in those pages. Each illustration she creates is a little piece of her childhood.

Lilith

Our programmer, was born with a calculator under her arm and a heart full of logic. Passionate about mathematics, she is the wise dragon who pulls the strings behind the scenes, bringing each story to life on the screen with her breath of code. Thanks to her, children can touch, explore, and immerse themselves in our stories.

Dani

Our animator, is the knight of our team. He adds sounds, creates animations, and brings characters to life. Since he was a child, he loved inventing special effects for his games, and now, with his sword of creativity, he ensures that each story is an unforgettable experience.
